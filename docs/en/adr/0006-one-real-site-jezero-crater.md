# ADR-0006 — One real site: Jezero Crater

> Portuguese version: [`docs/pt-BR/adr/0006-um-local-real-cratera-jezero.md`](../../pt-BR/adr/0006-um-local-real-cratera-jezero.md).

Status: accepted, 2026-11-14.

**Context.** The challenge suggests data from surface missions for the chosen site.
**Decision.** The base is in Jezero. Images and distances of the expedition sites come from Mars Trek and rover photos. High-resolution terrain models stay out of the MVP.
**Consequences.** Light data preparation. The map serves the expedition decision; it is not a routing system.
