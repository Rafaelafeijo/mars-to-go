# ADR-0025 — Asking Earth: a question about tomorrow, answered tonight

Status: accepted, 2026-11-14.

**Context.** Communication delay is one of the five themes (checklist B4). Real one-way signal delay Earth–Mars is about 4 to 24 minutes (NASA, DATA_MAP M9). A 10-second crisis is faster than any answer from Earth.

**Decision.** Today's question is about tomorrow; tomorrow's forecast shows today as "maybe". Asking costs 1 cell. The answer arrives after the plan is locked and appears in tonight's sol log with the signal travel time. Asking on sol 7 is useless and the log says why. In a 10-second crisis the button shows that the answer would take at least 8 minutes (2 × ~4 min) and is disabled. The answer is a prediction by a NASA model (DONKI WSA-ENLIL), and its data card says so.

**Consequences.** Information has a cost and a delay, never an instant benefit. The radio silence crisis needs no special rule: the disabled button is the lesson.
