# ADR-0015 — Mars first; parallels only with justification

> Portuguese version: [`docs/pt-BR/adr/0015-marte-primeiro-paralelos-so-com-justificativa.md`](../../pt-BR/adr/0015-marte-primeiro-paralelos-so-com-justificativa.md).

Status: accepted, 2026-11-14.

**Context.** Some of the data suggested by the challenge comes from the Space Station, the Moon or the Sun.
**Decision.** Every piece of data shows where it was measured. What is not from Mars goes in as a parallel, with the scientific justification recorded and the difference stated on the card. The CSA lunar material provides only general principles of growing crops in a closed environment.
**Consequences.** Some interesting data stays out for lack of a verified justification.
