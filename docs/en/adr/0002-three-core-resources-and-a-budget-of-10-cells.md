# ADR-0002 — Three core resources and a budget of 10 cells

> Portuguese version: [`docs/pt-BR/adr/0002-tres-recursos-centrais-e-um-orcamento-de-10-celulas.md`](../../pt-BR/adr/0002-tres-recursos-centrais-e-um-orcamento-de-10-celulas.md).

Status: accepted, 2026-11-14.

**Context.** The challenge asks for five decision themes, but warns that two or three core resources work better than five or six.
**Decision.** Resources: energy (10 cells per sol), air and food. The five themes become places to spend: Life Support, Greenhouse, Shield, Expedition, Ask Earth.
**Consequences.** The math fits within counting and adding. No distribution fills everything, which guarantees there is no free choice.
